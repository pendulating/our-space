# Design Context — our-space

**Product.** An interactive map of Manhattan that estimates how much of a walking
route is "watched" by the cameras and sensing devices entering public space
(fixed CCTV, ACE bus cameras, dashcams, smart glasses), plus citywide exposure
and a neighborhood-diversity equity overlay. Bevy/Rust → WASM/WebGPU; a DOM
chrome wraps the Bevy canvas with an in-canvas egui control panel.

**Audience.** The general public and civically-minded New Yorkers (non-experts),
plus researchers/advocates. It is a public-dissemination tool, not a dashboard.

**Use cases.** (1) Enter a walk A→B and feel how surveilled it is; (2) explore
the citywide exposure heatmap; (3) read the diversity↔camera equity story.

**Tone / personality — the core of the brief.** JOURNALISTIC / EDITORIAL, à la
The New York Times: SERIOUS, sober, considered — never playful, never an "app."
The frame is a broadsheet front page (masthead, standfirst, datelines, credit
lines) reporting honestly on a hard subject. Calm, grounded, honest, non-alarmist
— held in **stark contrast** to the cold, clinical surveillance it depicts.
Numbers are honest estimates with confidence tiers, never fear-mongering. (This
supersedes the earlier "earthy/organic/botanical" brief — the paper-and-ink base
stays; the warmth is now newsprint gravity, not soil/moss playfulness.)

**Typography — the division of labor.** A serif carries the EDITORIAL voice
(nameplate, headline, standfirst, article titles, pull quotes): Newsreader on the
web page, a serif-family display face in-canvas. Host Grotesk carries the
FURNITURE voice (kickers, labels, metadata, captions, UI controls) and is shared
with the egui panel. Serif-for-heds / gothic-for-labels is the newspaper division
of labor and is what carries the gravity. No emoji, no rounded "chips."

**Contrast strategy.** Chrome + base map = warm newsprint calm, crisp printed
rules, squared corners, one restrained iron-gall RUST accent (#9a3a16) used
sparingly. Surveillance layers (cameras, exposure heat, ACE corridors, confidence
ramps) = cold, hard, clinical data-viz that visibly *doesn't belong* on the warm
page — the tension is the message. Surveillance imagery in "related coverage" is
desaturated at rest, warming only on hover (held at arm's length).

**Constraints.** WebGPU/WASM; egui (in-canvas) + CSS (DOM chrome) + Bevy mesh
vertex/material colors share one palette. The map plate stays pure white (#fff =
Bevy ZINC_950); only the chrome bands are warm newsprint. Route stays
client-side. Large map → performance matters. Accessible, legible at a glance.

**Differentiation.** A serious newspaper's data desk reporting on the watched
commons — editorial gravity, not a civic-app dashboard, and emphatically not a
dark-mode neon surveillance UI. The warm printed page pierced by cold sensing.
