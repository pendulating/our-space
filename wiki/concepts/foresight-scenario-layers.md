# Foresight: The Layer That Is Arriving

Paper §6 (~1pp), under the CFP's *critical and sociotechnical foresight studies*
topic. Every number is a labeled **scenario**. No disparity claims.

| Layer | Spatial prior | Scenario parameters |
|---|---|---|
| Tesla Sentry | NYS DMV registrations by ZIP — 29,177 Teslas / 372 ZIPs | 4 veh/min peak, p_capture 0.30, 24/7 |
| Delivery robots | Robotability Score, 100 m grid (cite 3rd person) | 2/min peak, p_capture 0.50 |
| Smart glasses | none (ambient ped density) | 10 per 1,000 peds, p_record 0.05 |

## The argument (the payload, not the numbers)
These devices are privately owned, unregulated, and mobile. They escape the POST
Act (which reaches NYPD). They escape place-based notice: signage on a building
cannot notice a passing car. Their spatial signature is the inversion in
[[jacobs-inversion]]: the device concentrates where wealth is, but its subjects are
the workers who commute in. A Sentry Tesla parked in a wealthy block group watches
the home health aide, the delivery rider, the construction worker — people whose
residence is nowhere near the camera.

No residence-based measure can see this. That is the whole argument in one image.
See [[governance-gap]] and [[compounding-test-m1-m3]].

Tesla Sentry has no academic literature. Say so plainly; that absence is part of
the motivation.
