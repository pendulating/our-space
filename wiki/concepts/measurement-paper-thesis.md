# This Is a Measurement Paper

The instrument is the contribution. The paper does not claim that mobile sensors
compound the disparity. That claim is future work with a pre-registered design
([[compounding-test-m1-m3]]). The experiments have not run; the paper says so.

## What the paper claims
- Exposure is a trajectory property ([[point-vs-path]]).
- The exposure instrument is open and reproducible ([[exposure-instrument]]).
- Placement bias is real and robust ([[results-placement-bias]]).
- Lived exposure has equalized by saturation ([[results-equalization]]).
- Place-based governance cannot reach a moving camera ([[governance-gap]]).

## What the paper does not claim
- That mobile fleets compound the disparity. See [[foresight-scenario-layers]]
  for why speculative layers carry zero empirical weight.
- Any causal claim. Activity space is selected; the claim is descriptive and
  distributional (who is exposed), never causal.
