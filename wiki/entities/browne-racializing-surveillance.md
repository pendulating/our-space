# Browne — Dark Matters

**Simone Browne, *Dark Matters: On the Surveillance of Blackness* (Duke UP,
2015).** doi:10.1515/9780822375302 — source of "racializing surveillance."

Use the lantern laws: 18th-century New York required Black, mixed-race, and
Indigenous people to carry a lit candle after dark. That is a mobility-conditioned
surveillance regime — surveilled as a function of where you go and when, not where
you live. In New York City specifically.

This is the strongest answer to "why should FAccT care that this is about
mobility?" It grounds the trajectory claim in a centuries-long local history.
See [[jacobs-inversion]] and [[thesis-in-one-sentence]].
