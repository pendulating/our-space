# Kwan — Neighborhood Effect Averaging Problem

**Kwan, "The Neighborhood Effect Averaging Problem (NEAP)," *IJERPH* 15(9): 1841,
2018.** doi:10.3390/ijerph15091841

Because people move through many neighborhoods, mobility-based exposures average
toward the mean. This attenuates and can even nullify an apparent neighborhood
effect.

This is the single most valuable citation in the paper. It makes the equalization
finding ([[results-equalization]]) a predicted textbook NEAP result, not an awkward
null. Framing the contribution as "residence-based measures answer the wrong
question" makes the paper publishable whichever way the effect points.
