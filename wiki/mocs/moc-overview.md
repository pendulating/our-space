# Map of Content — Project Overview

`our-space` has one engine and two products.

1. **The civic tool.** A browser app. You enter a walking route in Manhattan.
   The app estimates how many cameras could see you, with time of day. See
   [[civic-tool]].
2. **The paper.** "Digital Eyes on the Street", submitted to FAccT 27
   ([[facct-27-deadlines]]). The paper is a measurement paper
   ([[measurement-paper-thesis]]). The engine is its instrument
   ([[exposure-instrument]]).
