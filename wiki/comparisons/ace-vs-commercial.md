# Statutory vs Latent Surveillance: ACE Buses vs Commercial Fleets

| Dimension | MTA ACE bus cameras | Nexar dashcams / Tesla Sentry / private ALPR |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | NY VTL §1111-c / §1111-c-1; mandated ≥50 mobile photo devices | None specific to fleet sensing |
| Purpose limitation | Adjudication only | Unrestricted |
| Retention | 1-year cap | Unregulated |
| Biometric / FRT | Banned outright | Unregulated |
| Access | MTA itself barred from images | Private owner control |
| Brayne frame ([[browne-racializing-surveillance]] companion) | Directed | Latent dragnet |
| Empirical status in paper | Observed trajectories; not yet in BG tables (M1 — [[compounding-test-m1-m3]]) | Scenario only ([[foresight-scenario-layers]]) |

**Verdict:** from the sidewalk the two classes are indistinguishable, yet their
accountability differs completely. This asymmetry is C5's sharpest exhibit and
motivates trajectory-based transparency ([[governance-gap]]). ⚠️ Verify current
ACE fleet counts by hand before citing (mta.info blocks fetching).
